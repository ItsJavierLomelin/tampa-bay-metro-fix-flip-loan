Questions before you submit

What makes a Town 'n' Country repair plan different?

Include exterior grade, drainage routes and documented water damage in the inspection file. Separate the cause of a repair from the cosmetic work that covers its visible effects. Include the records and photos behind the work you propose.

How should I support my Town 'n' Country resale estimate?

Use homes with comparable lot and water-exposure positions as well as interiors. An undamaged renovated sale does not answer your property's drainage questions. Explain the differences that remain between your house and each sale.

What should I confirm before choosing the scope?

Are there recorded drainage easements or unresolved water-entry problems at this address? Ask the relevant office or association about the actual parcel. Mark any unanswered question clearly when you submit.

Are you a direct lender?

No. We are connectors. We introduce investors to funding sources, and the funding source makes its own decisions and provides the actual terms.

Does submitting a project guarantee funding?

No. A submission starts a review and a possible introduction. It does not guarantee a match, an approval or a particular outcome.

Have a property in mind?

Tell us what you plan to buy and improve. We will review the details and connect you with funding sources without promising an outcome.
