## Local-copy revision

Each area has a researched narrative, property-specific repair and comparable-sales decisions, a local check and three local FAQs. Existing sections are used instead of adding another repetitive narrative block. No market-price, appreciation, deal-volume or approval claims are invented.

- tampa: Historic blocks need a different renovation plan.
- st-petersburg: Historic frontage belongs in the repair plan.
- clearwater: A North Greenwood rehab is not a beach-house comparison.
- dunedin: Downtown, the trail and the waterfront are distinct features.
- largo: Separate a downtown corridor from a subdivision rehab.
- safety-harbor: Match the renovation to its downtown setting.
- tarpon-springs: Downtown and Sponge Docks projects need a form-based look.
- gulfport: Beach Boulevard is a setting, not a citywide resale template.
- pinellas-park: A horse-trail setting is not a zoning shortcut.
- seminole: Lake Seminole proximity needs its own comparison.
- temple-terrace: Riverhills and the golf-course origins give the house context.
- plant-city: Historic exterior work needs more than a contractor allowance.
- brandon: Limona and the original town core need separate comparisons.
- riverview: The Alafia River and U.S. 301 shape the project setting.
- town-n-country: Drainage belongs in the scope, not behind new finishes.
- carrollwood: Original Carrollwood and the Village are different project settings.
- lutz: Separate a semi-rural lot from a planned-community flip.
- valrico: Keep suburban and rural settings distinct.
