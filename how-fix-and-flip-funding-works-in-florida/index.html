How Fix and Flip Funding Works in Florida

Fix and flip funding is short term financing built around an investment property rather than the borrower's paycheck. The property, the renovation plan and the planned resale carry the conversation. A funding source can still review credit, experience and other borrower details, but the deal itself is the starting point.

We are connectors. We introduce Tampa Bay investors to funding sources, and nothing on this page is a promise of approval or timing.

What funding sources look at

The property comes first. The address, current condition and contract terms tell a funding source what is being bought. The renovation scope shows what will be done, and comparable sales support what the finished home might sell for.

The exit matters as much as the purchase. Most fix and flip projects are repaid from the resale, so funding sources want to see how the after-repair value was estimated and how the home will be sold. Our project checklist shows how to put that package together.

How a project moves forward in Florida

You send the property details, the purchase terms and the renovation plan. We review them, ask for anything missing and introduce the project to funding sources that fit it. The funding source decides eligibility and documentation.

If an offer works for your project, the file moves toward closing. Florida closings typically run through a title company or attorney, and they can confirm the exact sequence for your file. Review the full written offer before deciding to move forward.

What a project usually needs

The funding source and the title company confirm exactly what your file requires. Across most fix and flip projects, the paperwork includes:

  • The signed purchase agreement for the property
  • A written scope of work and renovation budget
  • Recent nearby sales that support the planned resale
  • Entity documents if you are buying through an LLC
  • Proof of funds for any cash portion you will bring
  • Insurance information for the property during the project

Complete files move faster. Sending everything at the start keeps questions from surfacing right before closing.

Tampa Bay details worth checking early

Confirm the permit authority for your parcel, check flood information and obtain an insurance estimate. Historic review or substantial improvement rules may change the work, so bring unresolved property questions into the discussion early.

Common questions

Are you a direct lender?

No. We are connectors. We introduce investors to funding sources, and each funding source makes its own decisions and provides its own terms.

Do I need fix and flip experience?

Funding sources look at the whole file, and experience is one part of it. A clear renovation plan, a supported resale estimate and a realistic budget matter on every project, whether it is your first or your fifteenth.

How fast can funding happen?

Our goal is funding within 48 hours once the project is clear and the documents are complete. Actual timing depends on the property and the funding source, and no closing date is guaranteed.

Can I buy through an LLC?

Many investors buy through an entity. If you do, expect the title company and the funding source to ask for the entity documents. Your own legal and tax advisors can help you choose the structure.

Have a property in mind?

Tell us what you plan to buy and improve. We will review the details and connect you with funding sources without promising an outcome.
