Fix and Flip Project Checklist

A complete package is the fastest way to get a clear answer. This checklist covers what funding sources usually want to see and Tampa Bay items worth checking before you send anything.

We are connectors. We use what you send to introduce your project to funding sources, so the clearer the package, the smoother the conversation.

The property

  • Address, property type and number of bedrooms and baths
  • The signed contract or the offer you plan to make
  • Photos of every room, the roof, the basement and the exterior
  • Anything known about water, settlement, wiring or heating problems

The repair scope

  • A room-by-room list of work, with major systems named
  • Who will do the work and the timeline you expect
  • Permit needs, confirmed with the city or county office
  • Safety and structural items listed ahead of cosmetic ones

The resale plan

  • Three or more recent sales of similar homes nearby
  • Your estimate of the finished value and how you reached it
  • The buyer you expect: owner-occupant, investor or rental buyer
  • How long you expect the project to take from closing to sale

Tampa Bay items to check first

Confirm the property’s jurisdiction before contacting a permit office. Tampa lists Hyde Park, Seminole Heights, Tampa Heights and Ybor City among its local historic districts; designation and the planned exterior work determine which questions to ask. For a property in a flood hazard area, confirm how substantial improvement or damage rules affect your scope with the relevant city or county office.

Common questions

How detailed should my repair scope be?

Detailed enough that someone who has not seen the house can understand the work. A room-by-room list with the major systems called out works well.

What if I do not have contractor bids yet?

Say so and describe how you built your scope. It is better to flag what is still unconfirmed than to guess.

Where do I find nearby sales?

A real estate agent or the public records for the county can show recent sales. Pick homes of similar size and condition in the same city or neighborhood.

Have a property in mind?

Tell us what you plan to buy and improve. We will review the details and connect you with funding sources without promising an outcome.
